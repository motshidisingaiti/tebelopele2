How a visit begins

One conversation, four things handled

Tshedimoso is a navigation tool, not a diagnosis — it moves a client from question to the right service, and hands off to staff the moment something needs a person.

01

Ask

A client messages with a question, a symptom worry, or a request — in their own words, no menus to hunt through.

02

Understand

Tshedimoso matches the request to TWC and Ministry of Health-approved information, or the correct service pathway.

03

Act

Book, reschedule, or cancel an appointment; send a reminder; route a referral — logged straight into TWC's systems.

04

Escalate

Anything urgent or beyond scope is hand-carried to a counsellor or clinician, with the conversation history attached.

Data protection & safeguards

Care that stays confidential

Health information is sensitive by nature. Tshedimoso is built to Botswana's Data Protection Act and applicable health information and cybersecurity legislation.

  • 01  All platforms, AI models, APIs, cloud infrastructure and data processors are disclosed and reviewable by TWC.
  • 02  Client conversations and records are encrypted and access-controlled by staff role.
  • 03  The assistant never replaces clinical judgement — it routes, it does not diagnose.
  • 04  Escalation is automatic whenever a conversation touches something urgent or clinically significant.
  • 05  Integrations are scoped to what's approved, with a clear line between live and future access.
  • 06  TWC staff are trained on administration, privacy, and troubleshooting before go-live.
